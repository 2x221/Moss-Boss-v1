Moss Boss
Meteor addon module oriented for 1.21.11

An advanced Meteor Client addon for Minecraft 1.21.11 designed for high-efficiency automated moss and azalea expansion, intelligent inventory bone meal management, vegetation clearing for faster rates, and automated area spawn-proofing.

-Features
- Zero-Delay Bonemealing: High-speed automated moss and azalea conversion.
- Vegetation Clearing: Automatically breaks grass and ferns obstructing moss blocks.
- smart Bone Meal Crafting: Automatically unpacks bone blocks into bone meal based on a customizable inventory fill percentage limit.
- Crosshair Raycasting & FOV Filtering: Ensures the bot only targets blocks in front of you and never behind your back.
- Automated Spawn-Proofing: Automatically places full light blocks (glowstone, sea lanterns, etc.) in dark surrounding areas.
